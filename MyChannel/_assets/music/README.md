# Music

Drop ONE Content-ID-safe track here as `bed.mp3` — the assembler finds it, loops it, and ducks it
under the narration. Best free source: **YouTube Audio Library** (pre-cleared, monetizable).
Never use CapCut's built-in music (Content-ID claims).

Optional: put candidate tracks in `_auditions/` (and describe them in `_auditions/library.json`) to
audition them in the Studio (Scenes tab → 🎵 Music bed → "Use as channel bed").
